ABOUT DMLT

Digital Metaphor Literacy Toolkit is an educational project that helps learners recognise, interpret, and understand metaphors used in English digital communication.

💬 Real digital language 🔎 Context-based learning 🎮 Interactive practice

📘 What is DMLT?

The Digital Metaphor Literacy Toolkit (DMLT) was developed as part of the research project “Decoding Metaphors in English Social Media Comments”. It brings together digital and printable learning resources that help students move beyond literal translation and understand figurative meaning through context. Learners explore how metaphors communicate emotions, opinions, humour, and ideas in online conversations.

🎯

Our Aim

To support students in identifying, interpreting, and using metaphorical expressions in digital communication through authentic examples, guided strategies, and engaging learning activities.

WHY DMLT?

Online expressions cannot always be understood word for word. Context helps reveal what people really mean.

💡 The Challenge

Social media is full of creative expressions, internet slang, and figurative language. A learner may know every word in a comment but still misunderstand its message if it is interpreted literally.

🚀 Our Approach

DMLT turns metaphor learning into an active process: notice the expression, examine its context, identify its figurative meaning, explain it, and practise using it in communication.
