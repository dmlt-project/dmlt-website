DECODE THE METAPHOR!

Think beyond the words. Discover the hidden meaning.

Metaphors are like little puzzles. Follow these six detective steps to uncover what people really mean — especially in the fast-moving world of social media.

LOOK CLOSER. THINK DEEPER. Every word can be a clue. Use your detective skills to uncover the meaning behind the metaphor.

YOUR 6-STEP DECODING MISSION

Follow the clues. Connect the ideas. Crack the code!

STEP 01🔍

SPOT THE METAPHOR

Find the unusual expression.

Look for words or phrases that seem surprising or do not mean exactly what they say.

FIND THE CLUE
STEP 02💬

CHECK THE CONTEXT

Read between the lines.

Read the sentence or comment around the metaphor. The nearby words can give you important clues.

LOOK AROUND
STEP 03💡

THINK LITERALLY

Could this really happen?

Imagine the literal meaning. Ask yourself: “Can this expression happen in real life?”

QUESTION THE WORDS
STEP 04🧠

UNLOCK THE HIDDEN MEANING

What does the speaker really mean?

Think about the idea or feeling the speaker wants to express. Find the figurative meaning.

REVEAL THE MESSAGE
STEP 05📱

EXPLORE THE DIGITAL WORLD

Who says it, and where?

Consider the platform and audience. Social media culture, slang, trends and memes can shape meaning.

CHECK THE ONLINE CULTURE
STEP 06✍️

EXPLAIN IT YOUR WAY

Make the meaning clear.

Explain the metaphor in your own words. Rewrite it using simple, clear English.
